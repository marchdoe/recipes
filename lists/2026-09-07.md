## Notes

- **Fish tacos (Mon):** do NOT buy frying oil or beer for batter — pan-sear is the mod; skip those ingredients entirely.
- **Tzatziki (Tue):** buying store-bought (16 oz) is easiest since 1–2 cups are needed. If making from scratch, add 2 cups non-fat Greek yogurt, cucumber, garlic, and dill to the list; skip the separate tzatziki container.
- **Thai basil (Wed):** cilantro is a viable substitute if Thai basil isn't available — you'll already have cilantro from Monday.
- **Chicken (Thu + Sat):** can consolidate to one 2½ lb purchase of chicken breasts if shopping in one trip; separate as needed for prep nights.
- **Adobo can (Mon + Sat):** one 7-oz can covers both the Monday salsa (1–2 peppers) and Saturday chili (1 tbsp chopped) — no need for two cans.
- **Chili (Sat):** starts at room temperature, one pot, 45 min. Great for a batch-cook afternoon.
