## Notes

- **Mahi mahi** is recommended but any firm white fish works (cod, halibut, tilapia). Buy fresh if available — early July is a good season for mahi.
- **Cilantro:** two large bunches cover all five uses. If you prefer Thai basil for Thursday's noodles, grab one bunch cilantro + one bunch Thai basil instead.
- **Chipotle peppers in adobo:** one can covers both the fish tacos salsa (1–2 peppers) and the chili (1 tbsp chopped). Freeze remaining peppers in a zip-lock for future use.
- **Leftover plan:** Mon soup (6 servings) → Tue + Wed lunches. Sat chili (6 servings) → Sun dinner + Mon/Tue lunches next week; freeze any extra.
- **Mod reminder for street corn (Wed):** substitute Greek yogurt for both the mayo and sour cream to minimize saturated fat — the dish reads as equally creamy.
- **Mod reminder for Thai beef (Thu):** use low-sodium tamari and halve the fish sauce — this brings sodium from ~1,700 mg to ~1,100 mg/serving.
