## Notes

- **Tzatziki (Mon):** homemade is easy and heart-healthier — combine 1 cup 0% Greek yogurt, ½ grated Persian cucumber (squeezed dry), 1 minced garlic clove, 1 tbsp dill, 1 tsp lemon juice, salt.
- **Chickpea orzo (Sat):** Banza brand adds ~5 g protein and ~2 g fiber per serving vs. regular orzo — recommended if available at your store.
- **Sungold tomatoes (Sat):** peak mid-July; check the farmers market. Regular cherry tomatoes work equally well.
- **White wine (Sat):** any dry white (Sauvignon Blanc, Pinot Grigio, unoaked Chardonnay) works. The ½ cup can also be swapped for ½ cup additional chicken broth if preferred.
- **Prosciutto (Sat):** the heart-health mod is to skip it entirely — the dish is complete without it. If you want the savory crunch, 2–3 slices of Canadian bacon crisped in the pan is a lower-sodium, lower-sat-fat sub.
- **Monday leftovers:** the spiced chicken stores separately from the dressed greens. Keep them apart in the fridge so the kale doesn't wilt — recombine at serving. Stays fresh through Thursday.
