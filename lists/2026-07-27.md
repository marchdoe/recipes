## Notes

- **Chicken volume:** 3.5 lbs total — can buy one large pack and portion at home.
- **Tacos mod — no frying:** the fish taco recipe calls for beer batter and deep-fry. Skip this entirely. Season mahi mahi with chili powder and pan-sear 3–4 min per side in olive oil. No flour, beer, or frying oil needed — do not buy those.
- **Pesto quantity (Mon):** recipe calls for ½ cup but heart-health mod halves this to ¼ cup. Buy a small jar (6–8 oz) — leftover pesto keeps in the fridge.
- **Chipotle can (Tue + Sat):** one can covers both recipes. Use 1 pepper for taco salsa (Alisa: use ½ pepper) and 1 tbsp chopped for chili.
- **Broth:** Mon soup needs 8 cups (2 quarts); Sat chili needs 3–4 cups. Buying 3 quarts total gives some buffer for adjustments.
- **Leftover plan:** Mon soup (6 servings) → Tue + Wed lunches; Sat chili (6 servings) → Sun dinner + Mon/Tue lunches next week. Freeze extra chili portions.
- **Thai basil:** if your store carries it, use Thai basil in Thu's noodles for authenticity. Cilantro is a fine substitute.
