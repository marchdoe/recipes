## Notes

- **Monday modification reminder:** pan-sear the fish — do NOT deep-fry. Skip the flour, baking powder, beer, and frying oil entirely. Just season with chili powder and sear in a thin film of olive oil. The salsas are healthy as-is.
- **Wednesday egg modification reminder:** use 2 whole eggs + 4 egg whites (not 4 whole eggs as written). This cuts estimated cholesterol from ~280 mg to ~120 mg per serving.
- **Thursday pesto:** use only ¼ cup (half the recipe's ½ cup) to reduce sat fat. A good jarred pesto with less oil/dairy (e.g., Trader Joe's Genova pesto) works well.
- **Saturday brine:** Start Friday night Oct 23. Brine needs 8 hours refrigerator time. Pull pork from fridge ~30 min before searing Saturday morning.
- **Chicken quantity note:** buy 2½ lbs total and split: 1½ lbs for Tue tzatziki salad, 1 lb for Thu soup. Store Thu portion in fridge or freeze until Thursday.
- **Pesto soup crockpot option:** if Thursday is busy, combine all soup ingredients (except noodles/pesto/spinach/lemon) in the slow cooker Wednesday night, cook on low 6–8 hours, stir in pesto/spinach/lemon/noodles at dinner time.
