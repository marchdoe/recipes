## Notes

- **Monday pan-sear mod:** skip flour, baking powder, beer, and frying oil listed in the fish taco recipe. Season mahi mahi with chili powder only; sear in a thin film of olive oil 3–4 min per side.
- **Wednesday sodium mod:** buy low-sodium tamari (not regular soy); halve the fish sauce to 1 tbsp; substitute 1 tbsp olive oil for 1 of the 2 tbsp butter.
- **Thursday sodium mod:** halve the hoisin to ¼ cup; use less sauce when dipping individual pieces.
- **Saturday sat-fat mod:** reduce butter to 1–2 tbsp (from 4 tbsp); stir in only ½ cup cheddar, rest as a table sprinkle; top with Greek yogurt instead of sour cream.
- **Chicken broth:** Tue pesto soup and Sat chili both use bone broth/broth — buy together to save a trip. Three 32-oz cartons (96 oz total) covers both recipes with a little left over.
- **Chipotle can:** Mon salsa uses 1 chipotle pepper; Sat uses 1 tbsp chopped — one small (7 oz) can covers both. Freeze remaining peppers in an ice cube tray for future use.
- **Thai basil vs. cilantro (Wed):** fresh Thai basil is the authentic call and worth sourcing if your store carries it; cilantro is a fine substitute if not.
- **Broccoli doubles:** the 2 cups bought for Tue soup can yield an extra head for a quick roasted broccoli side on Thu alongside the BBQ wings.
