## Notes

- **Mahi mahi mod — grill, don't fry:** Skip the flour-and-beer batter entirely. Season fish with chili powder, salt, and lime; grill or pan-sear 3–4 min per side in 1 tbsp olive oil. Cuts estimated sat fat from ~10 g to ~4–5 g and calories from ~800 to ~500. The salsas are the stars — keep both.
- **Wednesday sodium mods are mandatory:** Low-sodium tamari (not regular soy) + 1 tbsp fish sauce (not 2) reduces estimated sodium from ~1,700 mg to ~1,000–1,100 mg. As written, this dish likely exceeds Doug's full daily sodium target at dinner alone.
- **Cilantro consolidation:** Two bunches cover all four nights that need cilantro (tacos ×2 uses, Thai noodles, chili topping). The tomatillo sauce uses the most — 1 full cup — so make sure both bunches are fresh.
- **Chicken consolidation:** Buy 4 lbs chicken breasts at once. Divide into 1 lb (Tue soup), 1½ lbs (Thu tzatziki salad), 1½ lbs (Sat chili). Store Thu and Sat portions in fridge or freeze if buying on Monday.
- **Broth consolidation:** 3 cartons of 32-oz broth covers both the soup (8 cups = 2 cartons) and chili (3–4 cups = ~1 carton). Bone broth preferred for extra collagen; regular chicken broth works fine.
- **Tzatziki sauce:** Store-bought saves time and is widely available; Costco's version offers great value. If making at home, use the Greek yogurt + cucumber + garlic + dill + lemon combo — it keeps 5 days refrigerated.
- **Thanksgiving Thursday:** If chicken-tzatziki-avocado-salad is not cooked Thursday, hold those ingredients for a future week — avocados (buy them slightly firmer if Thursday is uncertain), cucumber, kale, feta, tzatziki sauce, and chicken all keep 3–5 days refrigerated.
