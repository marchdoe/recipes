## Notes

- **Brine timing:** pork shoulder brine starts Friday night (Sep 25) — needs ~8 hours refrigerated before Saturday morning's sear and roast.
- **Chicken consolidation:** buy 2½ lbs chicken breasts in one purchase; portion 1½ lbs for Mon and refrigerate 1 lb for Thu (or freeze Thu portion if buying mid-week).
- **Fajitas yield:** 6 servings for 2 adults = Mon dinner + Tue lunch. Chicken + peppers reheat well; assemble fresh tortillas at lunchtime.
- **Salmon salad leftover:** roasted salmon keeps next-day for Wed lunch; the dressed salad does not — toss the greens only as needed.
- **Pork shoulder yield:** 10 servings for 2 adults = Sat dinner + Sun dinner + early Mon/Tue lunches. Freeze remaining portions — pork shoulder freezes very well.
- **Herb purchase:** rosemary, sage, and thyme are often sold as a "poultry herbs" 3-pack — one pack usually covers the pork shoulder recipe.
- **Juniper berries:** sold in the spice aisle of well-stocked grocery stores or online. If unavailable, omit — the brine still works without them.
